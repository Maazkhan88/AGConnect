# Product requirements

AG Connect is a bilingual, mobile-first, multi-brand platform for corporate identities, cards, QR codes, contact exchange, leads, campaigns, and analytics. AG Holding is the tenant group; brands are administrative and visual boundaries. Public visitors need no account.

## MVP outcomes

- Administer groups, brands, departments, teams, staff, templates, themes, cards, QR codes, leads, campaigns, files, approvals, roles, and audits.
- Resolve controlled group → brand → optional profile theme values.
- Publish fast `/p/[slug]` profiles, dynamic vCards, `/t/[token]` and `/q/[token]` redirects.
- Capture consented leads and privacy-conscious analytics with historical records.
- Support English/Arabic translations, LTR/RTL, and WCAG 2.1 AA practices.

## Acceptance boundary

A milestone is complete only when its required flows run and typecheck, lint, relevant tests, documentation, and build pass. Milestone 0 provides repository/tooling/docs and shells; it does not claim complete authentication or business workflows.
